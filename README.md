The Painfully Honest Out-of-Office — a tiny pure-stdlib Python program that writes one brutally honest but still polite out-of-office auto-reply, in a single sentence, to /output/entry.txt.

## The entry

> I am away until Monday, ignoring an inbox that grew faster than my will to live; your email is important to me in the exact proportion that my reply can wait until then.

169 characters — inside the 1–200 gate. It admits the true thing (the inbox is being ignored on purpose) while staying warm and professional enough to survive a forward to a manager: the sender is away, the reply waits, and the sender says so with a straight face.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
